What Can It Do?

Everything you need to manage your fridge smartly.

Add Items

Log any ingredient with its name, category, quantity, and expiry date.

Color-Coded Alerts

Items are highlighted based on how close they are to expiring.

Edit and Delete

Update quantities or remove items that have been used without refreshing the page.

Summary Dashboard

See how many items are fresh, expiring soon, or already expired at a glance.

Database Backed

All data is saved to a MySQL database through a Node.js backend.

Fully Responsive

Works on phones, tablets, and desktops of all sizes.

How It Works

Three simple steps to get started.

Step One

Open the Inventory Page

Navigate to the Inventory page from the menu to see all your stored items.

Step Two

Add Your Items

Fill in the form with the ingredient name, quantity, category, and expiry date.

Step Three

Track and Manage

Watch your items update with color-coded statuses and edit or delete anytime.
